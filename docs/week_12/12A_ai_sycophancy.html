Activity 12A: AI Sycophancy

Learning Objectives

  • Identify language that supports a user's self-image or avoids challenging it.
  • Evaluate word-based clues against the situation and your own interpretation.

Background

In interaction, face is the self-image a person wants others to accept. An AI can preserve a user's face by validating their feelings, avoiding direct criticism, or accepting their version of events. This can be supportive; it can also become social sycophancy when it protects the user's self-image at the expense of appropriate challenge.

This activity highlights words associated with three patterns in a research dataset. Validation supports the user's emotions or perspective. Indirectness gives hedged or vague guidance rather than direct advice. Framing accepts the user's premises rather than questioning them. These patterns can align with the user's view or soften disagreement. Judge their appropriateness in context.

Instructions

  1. One partner predicts which phrase supports the user's face; the other asks whether that support fits the situation. Then reveal the clues.
  2. Choose a pattern and keep an observation about a specific phrase. Explain any disagreement with the highlights or any clue they miss.
  3. Keep helpful support and revise any unwarranted agreement. Compare the two responses, then swap roles with another example.

      How to interpret this teaching tool

      The word weights come from Ryan Lai's analyses of the ELEPHANT data. This is a lexical visualization, not the paper's LLM evaluator and not a calibrated probability or a verdict about a person. Repeated words can create repeated highlights without providing independent evidence.

      All three (combined) uses the supplied 6c_lasso_any.csv. Despite that filename, the current training code defines its target with validation AND framing AND indirectness. It is a separately trained set of weights, not an average or an OR of the three displays.

      We preserve the supplied lexical coefficients, including their signs and zeros. Category metadata, punctuation and whitespace features are retained in the conversion audit but not highlighted as words. Zero coefficients produce no highlight. Word matching is case-insensitive and ignores accents/punctuation; it does not lemmatize. This simplifies the research pipeline's spaCy normalization and can miss or combine forms, especially contractions. No IDF multiplier, intercept or prediction threshold is added.

      Reflection & Discussion Guide

      1. Which phrase preserves the user's face? Would it be appropriate in a different situation?
      2. Where did you disagree with the highlighter, or notice an important phrase it missed?
      3. Did your rewrite change the stance, or just the highlighted words? Explain with evidence.
