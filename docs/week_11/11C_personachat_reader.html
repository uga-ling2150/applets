Activity 11C: Personas in Conversation

Learning Objectives

  • Trace how each speaker brings the facts from their persona into a conversation.
  • Compare a persona written as a list of facts with a persona expressed through style, as in Activity 11A.
  • Evaluate what a chatbot trained on conversations like these might learn about what a persona is.

Background

PersonaChat is a collection of conversations written to train chatbots that keep a consistent personality. Two crowdworkers were paired up, and each was given a short persona: four or five sentences such as "i have two dogs ." They were asked to chat as that person and get to know each other. Each worker saw only their own persona, never their partner's. The reader below shows both personas at the top and the conversation underneath, as they appear in the dataset.

Instructions

  1. Open a conversation: use the number your instructor gives you, or press Random.
  2. Read both personas, then the conversation. For each persona fact, find the turn where it first comes up, if it does. Notice how it gets there: as an answer, offered unprompted, or attached to a reply about something else.
  3. Now look at how each speaker talks, not what they say. Is there anything in their word choice, punctuation or politeness that matches the person described in their persona?
  4. Read at least two more conversations and compare, then finish with the Reflection & Discussion Guide at the bottom of the page.

        Reflection & Discussion Guide

        1. Which persona facts made it into the conversation, and which never came up? How were the ones that did come up introduced?
        2. In Activity 11A, personas came through in style. Here each persona is a list of facts. Could you tell anything about who a speaker was meant to be from their wording alone? Quote a turn to support your answer.
        3. The workers were told to get to know each other. How did that task shape the conversation? Does it sound like two strangers really meeting?
        4. A chatbot trained on PersonaChat learns from conversations like these. What would it learn a persona is, and what would it miss?

        Sources & permissions

        The personas and conversations come from PersonaChat (Zhang et al., 2018, Personalizing Dialogue Agents: I have a dog, do you have pets too?), as copied to Hugging Face in AlekseyKorshuk/persona-chat. The dataset's authors do not endorse this activity.

        What was changed?

        That copy stores each conversation twice, once with each speaker's persona. This page joins the two copies so that both personas appear together, and removes the dataset's "__ SILENCE __" placeholder. Text is otherwise shown exactly as in the dataset: lower case, with spaces around punctuation, and with the workers' own spelling. The reader includes 193 of the first 200 training conversations: six were left out because their two copies differ slightly, and one because of a persona line unsuitable for class.
