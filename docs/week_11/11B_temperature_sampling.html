How the probabilities are calculated

Base probability is the bigram count divided by the total count for this context. For temperature T > 0, the new weight is p raised to the power 1/T. Normalize these weights to sum to one. Top-k keeps the k highest probabilities; top-p then keeps the smallest prefix whose cumulative probability reaches p, after normalizing the top-k candidates. Finally, normalize the remaining weights again. Temperature zero uses greedy selection. This order describes the teaching simulation; provider implementations may differ.
