Activity 11A: Style and Persona

Learning Objectives

  • Identify language cues to persona.
  • Compare the effects of system prompts.

Background

A persona is the identity a speaker presents through language—for example, a careful tutor or an enthusiastic peer. A system prompt tells the model how to speak.

Here, both personas answer the same question. Look for differences in wording, such as cautious suggestions (“you could”) or enthusiastic expressions (“let’s!”). Use exact phrases to support your interpretation.

Instructions

  1. Generate two answers. Keep the default question and system prompts, then click Generate both outputs.
  2. Annotate a phrase. Select words in either answer, choose a language feature, and explain what the wording suggests. Click Add annotation.
  3. Compare and save. Keep the question fixed, change one system prompt, and generate again. Compare the wording, then download your comparisons and reflection notes.
