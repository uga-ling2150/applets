Activity 11A: Style and Persona

Learning Objectives

  • Identify language cues to persona.
  • Compare the effects of system prompts.

Background

A persona is the social identity a speaker projects through language, such as a careful tutor or an enthusiastic peer. A system prompt gives the model a role and speaking instructions; the user question is the task both personas answer. Look beyond whether the advice is correct: forms of address, hedges (such as “could”), vocabulary and expressions of enthusiasm can index a relationship, authority or attitude. These are interpretations supported by language evidence, not fixed personality measurements.

Instructions

  1. With a partner, keep the user question fixed and compare the two system prompts. Predict how their wording might differ, then generate both outputs (or open Offline comparison below).
  2. Select a phrase inside one output; it will appear in the annotation form. Choose a feature and explain what the phrase suggests about the speaker and relationship. Add the annotation, compare the evidence below the outputs, then swap roles and change one system prompt.
