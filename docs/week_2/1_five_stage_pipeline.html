Activity: Modular Conversational AI systems

Learning Objectives
  • Describe the five stages of the traditional conversational AI pipeline.
  • Locate where errors in the pipeline take place.
  • Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of this model.
Instructions

  2. If you encounter an error in the pipeline, write down where it occurred.

Reflection & Discussion Guide
Think about the following questions:
  • Did you produce any errors? At what stage did the error occur? Why do you think it occurred?
  • Try to 'break' the system in some way. Can you try to make the system 'break' at different stages?
  • What are the benefits and drawbacks of this multi-stage approach?
