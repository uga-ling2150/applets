Explicature and query rewriting

What does a short utterance leave unsaid? Make its meaning explicit, then compare your interpretation with a model’s.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify how pronouns and omitted words depend on conversational context.
  • Rewrite a human turn so that it can be understood on its own while preserving its meaning.
  • Evaluate whether your rewrite and a model’s rewrite make justified interpretations.
Background: making meaning explicit

An utterance can communicate more than its words state on their own. Context helps us identify referents and fill in omitted information. In this activity, query rewriting makes some of that context-dependent meaning explicit.

After “I’m deciding between a red bag and a blue bag,” the reply “The blue one” may become “I choose the blue bag.” This is an interpretation of the utterance, not an answer to it. Query rewriting illustrates aspects of explicature; it does not capture every kind of pragmatic interpretation.

Sometimes the context leaves more than one interpretation open. A good rewrite should not invent information to hide that uncertainty.

Instructions

  1. Use the example or write both sides of your own conversation.
  2. For each Human turn, write a standalone version before revealing the model’s version. An already complete utterance may stay unchanged.
  3. Compare the versions and explain your judgment. The model’s version is not an answer key.

    Reflection & Discussion Guide

    1. Which pronouns or omitted expressions depended on earlier turns? Identify the exact words that supported your interpretation.
    2. Did you and the model express the same meaning with different wording? Could both be acceptable?
    3. Did either version add information that was not established, or change a request into a question?
    4. When the context is ambiguous, what clarification would you ask for?
