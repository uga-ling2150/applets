Activity: Reflecting on Winograd schemas

Learning Objectives

  • Describe the structure of a Winograd schema
  • Explain the reasoning that underlies responses to Winograd schemas
  • Evaluate the extent to which correct answers to Winograd schemas are dictated by cultural assumptions and norms that may not be universal

Background

A Winograd schema is a common type of question used in AI benchmarks to evaluate language models in terms of their ability to determine coreference. This activity will examine some of its assumptions.

Instructions

  1. Select what you think is the correct answer to each Winograd schema.
  2. Write down why you think that answer was correct.
  3. Look at the correct answer provided, and compare it to your own answer

Reflection & Discussion Guide

Think about the following questions:
  1. Were there any situations where your answers or explanations diverged with the one given? If so, why?
  2. Compare your answers with your groupmates'. Did anyone arrive at a different answer or explanation? If so, why?
  3. If your answers to 1-2 are both no, can you think of how a reasonable person might still come up with a different answer or explanation?
