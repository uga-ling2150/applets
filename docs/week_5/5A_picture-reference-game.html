Activity: Picture Reference

Learning Objectives

  • Describe what a referring expression is, and how speakers pick out one specific thing among similar alternatives using words alone
  • Explain why referring expression comprehension can be difficult for AI systems to achieve, including cultural and linguistic factors

Background

AI systems can look at images or documents have to figure out exactly which thing a person means when they refer to something in what they've uploaded. In this activity, you'll experience that same challenge from the human side: one of you (the Describer) sees a target object among two similar-looking alternatives and has to describe it in words; the other (the Guesser) only has that description to go on. A few rounds add an extra wrinkle: a specific word you're not allowed to use, forcing you to find another way to refer to the same thing.

Instructions

  1. Two students share this screen, taking turns.
  2. The Describer sees three real objects. One is marked as the target. They write a short description that would let someone pick it out. In a couple of rounds, one specific word is off-limits — you'll be told which.
  3. The device is passed over. The Guesser reads the description (without knowing which one was the target) and clicks the photo they think it matches.
  4. You'll swap roles each round. There are 5 rounds, so everyone gets a close-to-equal turn at both jobs.

Picture Reference

One of you looks at a real object and describes it in your own words. The other only hears the description and has to spot the right one among two convincing look-alikes. It's the same everyday skill as saying "the one on the left" — and it's trickier than it sounds.

Ready to start.

Reflection & Discussion Guide

Think about the following questions:
  • When constructing descriptions, what did you do to ensure that your friend could understand what you were saying?
  • Did you run into any situation where a reasonable person could have produced a description that confused another person - possibly from a different community?
  • Do you think a description that worked for your partner would also work for a system like SHRDLU looking at the same three photos? For each of the questions, what kind of knowledge do modern AI systems need to comprehend the expression?
