Activity 7A: Backchannel Locations

Learning Objectives

  • Identify possible backchannel locations.
  • Compare response timing across listeners.
  • Explain patterns using wording, pauses and intonation.

Background

Backchannels such as “mm-hmm” or “yeah” acknowledge a speaker without taking over. Mark when you would respond, then compare with your class. There is no single correct answer.

Instructions

  1. Join using your teacher’s link or code.
  2. Listen independently with headphones. Press Backchannel now when you would respond; Space or Enter works when that button is focused.
  3. Listen to the end to submit, then compare after your teacher releases the results.
